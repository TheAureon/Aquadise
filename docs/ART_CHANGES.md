# Art pass (Graphics stage 4)

Every sprite below was redrawn by `tools/gen-placeholders.js --force` after the art pass in
`src/placeholder-art.js`. Sizes, frames, anchors and hitboxes are unchanged (the only sprite whose visible
bounds changed is the UFO, which gained a glass dome on top). The originals are in git history: commit
`c58d83e~1` (the commit just before the art pass) has them at the same paths.

## To put an original back

1. Get it from git history: `git checkout c58d83e~1 -- assets/sprites/<path>`.
2. In `assets/manifest.js`, add `"custom": true` to its entry, so `gen-placeholders.js --force` never redraws it.
3. Run `node tools/embed-sprites.js` (the copy the game uses when opened from a file).

To put them all back: `git checkout c58d83e~1 -- assets/sprites` and run `node tools/embed-sprites.js`.
(Without `--force` the generator never overwrites an existing PNG.)

## What changed

- **Everything with an outline** (creatures, plants, decor, the chest, bait, signpost, console, beam pad):
  one shared light from the top-left (`Pix.light`): edges facing left catch a little light, edges facing
  right fall into shade, and the bottom-right corner gets a faint cool rim. The outline stays dark.
- **Hand-touched** (the most visible):
  - the player diver: a glint on the visor and a shaded back of the helmet, swimming and standing;
  - every fish-shaped creature (`fish`; includes 4 of the 10 most common: Fanray Damsel, Porthole Darter,
    Ember Goby, Glasswinged Minnow): a gill line, a small side fin that sways frame to frame, a shine on the back;
  - snails (Drift Snail, Coral Whelk...): a shine on the shell, the eye stalk nods now and then;
  - crabs (Knuckle Crab...): a shine on the shell, claws that pinch (a notch opens on alternate frames);
  - jellies (Open Drifter...): a shine on the bell;
  - limpets (Vent Limpet) and barnacles (Barnacle Crawler): small shines;
  - the UFO: a glass dome lit from the top-left and little chase lights along the rim (held still with REDUCE FLASHING);
  - the station's hull tile: rivets lit from the top-left;
  - the title logo (drawn in `src/title.js`, not a sprite): each letter's left edges catch light and its
    bottom edges sit in a cool shade.
- **Fixed:** two shapes were both called `bottle`, so the Message Bottle decoration would have been redrawn
  as the beach bottle on the next regeneration; the decoration's shape is now `bottlelying` (its picture is
  unchanged; its recorded visible bounds now match it).
