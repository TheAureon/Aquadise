# Trying the game on a real device

A short checklist for an iPhone, an iPad and an Android phone. It can't be done from a computer, because
phones handle sound, touch, saving and downloads in their own ways. Each step says what to do and what you
should see or hear. Anything that doesn't match is worth a note: which device, which browser, and what
happened instead.

**Before you start**
- Open the game in the phone's normal browser (Safari on iPhone / iPad, Chrome on Android).
- **iPhone and iPad:** flip the **silent switch off** (or, on newer iPhones, turn Silent Mode off in
  Control Center). With it on, iOS mutes all web page sound, and the game can't change that. Turn the
  volume up.
- Try it both ways: in the browser, and added to the home screen (Share > Add to Home Screen).

## 1. Starting and sound
1. Load the page. **See:** the title screen fills the screen, clear of the notch and the home bar.
2. Tap once anywhere. **Hear:** the title music starts within a second.
3. Lock the phone, wait 10 seconds, unlock it. **Hear:** silence while locked; the music comes back after
   your next tap (or at once).
4. Switch to another app and back. **Hear:** the same: silent while away, back when you return.
5. If you can: take a call, or let an alarm or timer ring, then go back to the game and tap. **Hear:** the
   music comes back. (This is the "interrupted" case on iPhone, the most likely to go wrong.)
6. In the settings' SOUND tab, move the music and sound sliders, then mute. **Hear:** each one changes at once.

## 2. Touch and the screen
1. Start a game. **See:** the joystick on the left and the buttons on the right, inside the safe area.
2. Swim, swing the net, sneak, use the hydrophone (LISTEN). **See:** each control responds at once, with
   no page scrolling, zooming or text selection.
3. Turn the phone sideways and back. **See:** the game resizes and the controls move to fit, within a
   moment.
4. Open the log, the map and the pause menu by touch, and scroll a long list. **See:** they scroll and
   close normally.
5. Pinch and double-tap on the game. **See:** the page never zooms.

## 3. Saving and backups
1. Play a minute, close the tab completely, open the game again. **See:** CONTINUE brings you back where
   you were.
2. MENU > SAVE & BACKUP > BACK UP MY GAME. **See:** the share sheet (iPhone: "Save to Files"; Android: Drive, Files...)
   or a download. Save it.
3. MENU > SAVE & BACKUP > RESTORE A BACKUP, and pick that file. **See:** the game says it's restored.
4. COPY SAVE CODE, paste it into Notes, then copy it back and use PASTE SAVE CODE. **See:** it opens.
   Also try a code made on a computer: it should open on the phone as well (older Safari included).

## 4. Pictures and the music
1. In a tank, take a photo. **See:** the photo is saved, or the browser offers to download it (iPhone may
   ask first, then put it in Files > Downloads).
2. Open the jukebox and play a song, pause it, play it, skip. **Hear:** each works without a click or a
   gap of more than a moment.
3. If you have added songs of your own (assets/music), play one in the jukebox. **Hear:** it plays after
   at most a second's wait, at about the same loudness as the built-in songs.

## 5. Slow devices
1. Play in the sea for a few minutes, including at night and in a cave. **See:** it stays smooth enough to
   play. On an older phone a toast may suggest LOW EFFECTS (only once).
2. Turn LOW EFFECTS on (SETTINGS > OPTIONS). **See:** lighter lighting and fewer particles; it should feel
   smoother. **Hear:** the music is a little thinner but never cuts out.
3. Leave the game running for 20 minutes (the jukebox playing is a good test). **See:** no slowdown, no
   reload by itself. (A page that reloads on its own usually ran out of memory: worth noting.)

## What to send back
For anything that went wrong: the device and its system version (Settings > General > About), the
browser, the step number, and what happened. A screen recording helps a lot.
